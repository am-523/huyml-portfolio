Approach (The 7 Stages)

P1. Discovery

Align on the brief, scope, budget and timeline.

P2. One page first

Usually the homepage to establish the direction.

P3. Build the rest

Complete remaining pages with feedback and iterations.

P4. Mobile matters

Adapt and refine the experience for mobile screens.

P5. Final review

Polish everything before development.

P6. Pixels to production

Ensure the live site performs identically to design.

P7. Launch 🏆

Deploy to the web and pray for some awards.
